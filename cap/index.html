Capstone, the final station

Ship an AI feature end to end

Take one AI feature from problem to launch plan, using everything from the gym. Your "Ship it" plans from each station are already most of the work: this page gathers them into one portfolio piece you can share, present in an interview, or bring to your team.

The brief

Pick one AI feature: ideally a real one from your own product, or Ledgerly's AI help assistant if you'd rather practise. Then work through the nine deliverables. Each builds on a station you've done; follow the link back if you need a refresher. Your ticks and notes stay in this browser.

Review it like a hiring manager

Before you share it, check your capstone against these five questions. They're the ones experienced AI product leaders ask.

  1. Is the problem worth solving with AI? A real user, a real cost today, and a clear reason a rule or a form wouldn't do.
  2. Is "good" defined before it was built? Examples, a golden set and pass rates per failure type, including zero-tolerance ones.
  3. Is the evidence real? Eval results, a judge with a measured catch rate, and a prototype people actually tried.
  4. Are risk, trust and cost handled? Guardrails, the right autonomy level, a cost per task that leaves a margin, and the compliance basics.
  5. Can the launch be measured and stopped? Stages, an outcome metric, guardrail metrics and a kill switch.

Where to next

  1. Ship it for real. Bring your spec and eval to your team; offer to own the golden set.
  2. Keep a practice habit. Replay the games, and revisit the expert shelves as the field moves.
